Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what Zaply collects, why, and the rights you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and applicable laws.

1. What we collect

  • Identity & contact: full name, email address, phone number.
  • Transaction data: purchases, wallet funding, receipts and references.
  • Security data: hashed passwords and PINs (we never store them in plain text), session tokens, device and log data used to detect fraud.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and verify your email.
  • To deliver airtime, data, electricity tokens and cable subscriptions you purchase.
  • To process wallet funding through our payment gateway partners.
  • To provide receipts, support, fraud prevention and service improvements.

3. Card details

Zaply does not see or store your card numbers. Funding is handled on checkout pages hosted by our PCI-DSS compliant payment partners; we only receive a payment reference and status.

4. Who we share with

Only what is necessary: VTU aggregators and utility billing systems (to fulfil purchases), payment processors (to move money), and regulators where required by law. We never sell your personal data.

5. Storage & retention

Data is stored on secured infrastructure with encryption in transit. Transaction records are retained as required by financial regulations; other data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above.

6. Your rights

  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent (e.g. optional notifications).
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, email privacy@zaply.ng. We respond within statutory timeframes.

7. On your device

The app stores only your sign-in session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Your transaction PIN is never stored on your device.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will notify you in-app before it takes effect.
